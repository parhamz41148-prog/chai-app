Common questions

Why are my prayer times a little different from my synagogue's calendar?

Communities follow different methods. In the More tab, under prayer times, you can choose GRA or Magen Avraham, your candle-lighting minutes and your Havdalah time. Times are calculated for sea level, so mountains and tall buildings can shift them by a minute or two.

The Jerusalem compass points the wrong way.

Calibrate your phone's compass by moving it in a figure-eight, and keep it away from metal, magnets and phone cases with magnets.

How does Shabbat mode work?

When it's on, Chai locks itself before Shabbat and Yom Tov and holds its notifications until after Havdalah. It only controls Chai, not the rest of your phone.

Can I read without an internet connection?

The calendar, prayer times and compass work offline. Texts you have opened or saved, including this week's Torah portion from the Shabbat checklist, are kept for offline reading.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Chai keeps everything on your phone and has no account, so your data isn't stored anywhere else. Your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup may include it.

Does Chai give halachic rulings?

No. Chai helps you practice; ask your rabbi about your own situation.
