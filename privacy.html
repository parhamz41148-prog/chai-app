Chai

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Chai does not collect personal data. Everything you enter stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no servers that store your data, no analytics and no advertising.

What stays on your phone

  • Your tradition, heritage, language and display settings
  • Personal dates such as yahrzeits, Hebrew birthdays and anniversaries
  • Your prayer journal, prayer tracker, checklists, tzedakah log and other notes
  • Texts you save for reading offline

This information is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Location

If you allow it, Chai uses your location to calculate prayer times (zmanim), candle lighting and Havdalah, and the direction of Jerusalem. The calculation happens on your phone, and your location is not sent to us or stored anywhere else. You can use the app without location by choosing a city, and you can turn location access off at any time in your phone's settings.

Notifications

Reminders, for example before candle lighting or the latest time for the morning Shema, are scheduled on your phone. They are not sent from a server.

Texts from Sefaria

Prayer and study texts are downloaded from Sefaria, a non-profit library of Jewish texts. These requests ask only for the text being opened, with no personal information attached. As with any website, Sefaria's servers can see your device's IP address when a text is requested; Sefaria's own privacy policy covers how they handle it.

Links to other services

Some features open other apps or websites, such as your maps app (to find synagogues, schools and kosher food nearby) or websites with Torah classes. Once you leave Chai, that service's privacy policy applies.

Purchases

Chai is sold through the Apple App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details.

Children

Chai does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
